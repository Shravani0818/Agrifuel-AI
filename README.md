# 🌱 AgriFuel AI

### Smart Farming. Smarter Residue. Cleaner Energy.

AgriFuel AI is an AI-powered smart agriculture platform that connects **farm intelligence, crop health, residue management, and community bioenergy** in one system.

The platform helps farmers make better decisions about their crops and irrigation, while turning post-harvest crop residue into a potential community energy resource.

---

## 💡 The Idea

Farmers face multiple connected challenges:

- Crop residue is often treated as waste or burned.
- Irrigation and crop-care decisions can depend on guesswork.
- Farmers need simple, accessible agricultural guidance.
- Communities need better ways to track shared bioenergy resources.

AgriFuel AI connects these problems into one technology ecosystem.

> **From Soil to Shared Power.**

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🤖 AI Crop Doctor
Farmers can upload a crop image and receive an AI-based analysis of visible crop issues.

- Crop identification
- Visible symptom analysis
- AI-powered recommendations
- Organic-first treatment suggestions
- Chemical treatment only when necessary
- Confidence-based AI insights
- Multilingual support

---

### 🌦️ Smart Farm Advisor

AgriFuel AI combines farm and environmental information to provide personalized farming guidance.

- Soil moisture
- Temperature
- Humidity
- Rain probability
- Weather information
- Crop type and growth stage
- Irrigation recommendations

The system helps farmers decide **when irrigation may be needed and when it may be better to wait**.

---

### ♻️ Smart Residue Intelligence

After harvest, the platform estimates crop residue based on farm area and crop type.

It connects residue data to the community bioenergy system.

Example workflow:

**Farm → Crop → Residue → Collection → Community Biogas → Energy → Farmer Energy Account**

The system can estimate:

- Crop residue
- Collectible biomass
- Potential biogas
- Potential energy generation
- Farmer energy contribution

---

### ⚡ Community Biogas & Energy Tracking

AgriFuel AI supports a shared community biogas model where farmers contribute crop residue to a common bioenergy system.

The platform tracks:

- Residue contribution
- Biogas generation
- Energy generated
- Energy consumed
- Remaining energy balance
- Farmer energy credits
- Fair-share allocation

This creates a transparent digital record of community energy usage.

---

### 📡 IoT Monitoring

The prototype includes an IoT monitoring layer designed around:

- ESP32
- Soil moisture sensor
- DHT22 temperature/humidity sensor
- MQ-4 methane sensor
- Relay module
- Irrigation pump control

The IoT layer provides sensor information that can support farm and energy-management decisions.

---

### 📱 Offline SMS Alerts

Important alerts can be sent through GSM SMS when mobile internet is unavailable.

Example triggers include:

- **90% biogas usage → Warning**
- **95% biogas usage → Urgent alert**
- **100% usage → Critical alert**
- Heavy rainfall warnings
- Low soil-moisture alerts
- Irrigation recommendations

> **No internet? Important alerts can still reach the farmer through SMS, provided cellular service is available.**

---

### 🌍 Multilingual Farmer Experience

The platform supports:

- 🇬🇧 English
- 🇮🇳 Hindi
- 🇮🇳 Marathi

The selected language is designed to apply across the application interface and AI-generated recommendations.

Voice playback is also included for supported AI recommendations.

---

### 📊 Regional Agri Intelligence Dashboard

A unified regional dashboard provides a broader view of agricultural activity.

It can display:

- Participating farmers
- Crop health indicators
- Estimated crop residue
- Biogas potential
- Village-level risk indicators
- Community bioenergy information

This can help FPOs and relevant decision-makers understand regional agricultural trends.
